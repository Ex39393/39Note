39Note policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

39Note is a local-first PDF reading and study workspace. Most application data stays in the browser profile on the device you use. Network features are optional or occur only when you invoke a feature that needs an external service.

In short: 39Note has no advertising account, does not sell personal information, and version 1.0.0 contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDK. Optional Google Drive sync, online dictionary lookups, and a user-configured AI provider each have the limited data flows described below.

1. Scope

This policy describes the 39Note web application, its optional Google Drive sync feature, and the small 39Note authentication service used to connect Google Drive. It does not replace the privacy policies of Google, Cloudflare, Wikimedia, NCBI, an AI provider you choose, your browser, or your device.

2. Website hosting

The production web application is served by GitHub Pages. When you load the site, GitHub can process ordinary web-request and usage metadata such as your IP address, device and browser information, requested page, referring page, and the date and time of the request. 39Note does not add its own analytics or advertising tracker to this hosting traffic. GitHub processes this information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. Data kept on your device

39Note uses browser storage, including IndexedDB, local storage, and session storage, to provide the application. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

  • imported PDFs and locally converted PDF copies, document fingerprints and identifiers, annotations, Notes, links, collections, reading position, and organization metadata;
  • print drafts, local backups and restore metadata, application preferences, and temporary diagnostic or recovery state;
  • AI conversations, prompt profiles, provider settings, and—only if you choose to remember it—the API key for your selected AI provider; and
  • a personal-device session token for optional Google Drive sync, or a session-only token when you select temporary/public-device mode.

Google access tokens are held in browser memory only. Raw OCR recognition output is session-only; records you explicitly create from recognized text follow the normal persistence rules for annotations, Notes, links, and backups.

4. Optional Google Drive sync

Google Drive sync is off until you connect a Google account. 39Note requests only the drive.file permission. This lets the application work with files it creates or that you explicitly open with it; it does not grant general access to every file in your Drive.

When enabled, original PDFs and allowlisted editable study records move directly between your browser and a visible 39Note folder in Google Drive over HTTPS. Those records may include annotations, Notes, organization and reading state, print drafts, AI conversations, and safe AI settings. AI API keys, passwords, Google client secrets, custom authentication headers, and Google refresh tokens are excluded from Drive sync payloads.

Authentication service metadata

A Cloudflare Worker and D1 database act only as an OAuth and device-session broker. The broker does not receive PDFs, annotations, Notes, AI conversations, or Google Drive file or folder identifiers. It processes and stores limited account and authorization metadata:

  • Google's opaque Drive permission identifier, granted scope, timestamps, and revocation status;
  • an encrypted Google refresh token, together with its random initialization vector and encryption-key version (the encryption key is stored separately);
  • random device/session identifiers and only a SHA-256 hash of each issued device-session token; and
  • one-time, expiring OAuth state and authorization-grant metadata.

Revoked authorization and session rows may remain as fail-closed status and audit metadata. Disconnecting a device does not delete your browser library or your Drive files. “Disconnect all devices” revokes 39Note sessions and submits Google token revocation, but it is separate from deleting Drive content.

Google API data

39Note's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide and secure the Drive sync functionality you request. It is not used for advertising, sold, or transferred for unrelated purposes.

5. Other optional network features

Dictionary lookups

39Note ships an offline WordNet dictionary. When an online definition lookup is used, the selected or normalized term may be sent directly from your browser to English Wiktionary (Wikimedia) and NCBI MeSH. Their services can receive ordinary request metadata such as your IP address and browser headers.

User-configured AI

39Note does not provide an AI account and does not operate an AI relay. If you configure and use an AI provider, your question, conversation context, and selected or retrieved document excerpts are sent directly from your browser to the endpoint you selected. The provider receives the API credential and request metadata needed to answer. Its own terms and privacy policy apply.

AI credentials and custom authentication headers stay in the browser profile and are not synced. If you choose “Remember API key,” the key remains in browser local storage until you clear it; otherwise it is kept only in the browser session.

6. Retention and your controls

  • Local data remains in the relevant browser profile until you remove it in 39Note or clear that site's browser data. A downloaded backup remains wherever you save it.
  • Synced content remains in Google Drive until you use an explicit 39Note Drive-removal action or manage it in Google Drive. Local quarantine or “ignore” actions do not claim to delete cloud records.
  • You can disconnect the current device, disconnect all devices, revoke 39Note from your Google Account, clear AI credentials, and stop using optional network features.

Browser storage and Google Drive are separate. Removing one copy does not necessarily remove the other. Review the wording of a removal action before confirming it, and keep an independent backup of important work.

7. Security

39Note uses HTTPS for network traffic, OAuth state and one-time exchange values, scoped Google authorization, hashed device-session tokens, and AES-256-GCM encryption for stored Google refresh tokens. No system can guarantee absolute security. Browser extensions, device users, the browser profile, and third-party services you connect can access data within their own permissions.

8. Children's privacy

39Note is a general study tool and is not directed to children under the age at which parental consent is required in their location. Do not connect a child's account or submit a child's personal information without any consent required by applicable law and the connected service.

9. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected on this page by updating the date above. A new release may also describe changed data flows in its release notes.

10. Contact

For a privacy question or request, use the 39Note issue tracker. Do not include document content, credentials, account identifiers, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
