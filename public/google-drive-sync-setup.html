Persistent personal Google Drive sync

39Note requests only drive.file. PDFs and editable study data travel directly between this browser and Google Drive—not through the authentication service.

Connection

Choose Connect Google Drive in the toolbar. After the first approval, this browser stores an independent revocable device authorization and can reconnect after reload or browser restart without another prompt.

If 39Note says Persistent sync backend not configured, this build needs the public VITE_39NOTE_SYNC_AUTH_URL setting. Google client IDs and secrets cannot be entered in the browser.

What is stored where

  • Google Drive: original PDFs and allowlisted editable study data in a visible 39Note folder.
  • Authentication service: an AES-256-GCM-encrypted Google refresh token, an opaque account ID, and hashed/revocable device-session metadata.
  • This browser: local library data and its device-session token. Google access tokens stay in memory only.

Drive content uses HTTPS and Google Drive storage but is not end-to-end encrypted by 39Note. AI API keys, passwords, Google client secrets, and custom authentication headers never sync.

Devices and revocation

Open Authorized devices to inspect the device count, switch Google accounts explicitly, disconnect only this browser, or disconnect all devices and revoke the Google grant.

Offline and outages

39Note remains usable locally. Changes stay queued while offline or while the authentication service is unavailable.
